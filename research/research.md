# Research — Nestwood

Status: **not started** (phase 2, after concept). See open questions in [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## Scope

- Tech stack evaluation for the mobile-first frontend
- Competitor audit: AllTrails, Komoot, Google Maps AI trip planning — what their 2026 AI assistants cover and where the multi-day gap actually is
- Data source research: Kartverket, Lantmäteriet, UT.no/DNT — API shape, licensing (CC0), coverage, gaps
- Benchmark: how each competitor's single-day AI flow compares against the multi-day flow Nestwood needs to prove
- Visual/brand direction — whether the previous cycle's light Scandinavian-minimalist style carries over
